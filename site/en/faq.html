Common questions

Before you call.Short answers.

How long does a renovation take?

It depends on the size and the work involved. The deadline goes into the quote after the site visit, so you know the date in advance.

Can I live in the home during the renovation?

With a partial renovation it is often possible. For a full renovation we recommend the home is empty. We discuss it at the site visit.

Do you buy the materials?

We can handle the supply, or work with materials you choose. We help you pick flooring, tiles and windows.

How do I pay?

In stages set out in the contract. You pay for completed work, not everything up front.

Do you work outside Varna?

We work in Varna and the region. Call us and we'll tell you whether we cover your address.

Ready for a site visit?

We come to you, take measurements and give you a written quote.
